Where biology meets computational thinking.

I'm a computational biologist with a PhD in Computational Biology and experience applying bioinformatics, statistics and machine learning to biomedical research, with a particular focus on cancer genomics.

My work spans methylation-based biomarker discovery, next-generation sequencing analysis, predictive modelling and reproducible research pipelines. At the Institute of Cancer Research, London, I worked on translational cancer research, including targeted ctDNA methylation analysis.
